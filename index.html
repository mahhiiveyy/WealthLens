Getting started

Up and running in
under 2 minutes.

01

Connect your broker

Tap Zerodha, Groww, or Upstox and log in with your existing credentials via secure OAuth. Read-only access — we can never trade or move money.

02

Portfolio loads instantly

Your entire holdings, P&L, and sector breakdown appear automatically — no manual entry, no spreadsheets. Live data, zero friction.

03

Set a goal

Add a financial goal — home, vacation, emergency fund. WealthLens calculates your monthly SIP and tracks progress as a visual tile.

04

Glance, don't obsess

The home screen widget shows your portfolio health in 3 seconds. A Sunday digest covers the week. Designed to be checked less, not more.
